Dot Garden

Link every pair of flowers

Draw a path between each pair of matching flowers. Paths cannot cross, and every plot must be filled.

  • Touch or mouse: Drag from a flower to its match. Drag back to shorten a path.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move. Space or Enter picks up or drops a path, and arrows draw while it is held. R restarts.
