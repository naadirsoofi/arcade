Pick the orchard clean

Launch fruit into the canopy. Three or more of a kind pop, and anything left hanging falls for bonus points. The canopy drops a row every few shots, so clear it before fruit reaches the launcher.

  • Touch Slide a finger anywhere to aim, lift to fire. Tap the next fruit to swap.
