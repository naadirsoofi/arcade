Seven Stacks

Build four suits

Move every card to the four foundations, each suit from ace up to king. On the seven piles, stack cards downward in alternating colors. Only a king can start an empty pile.

  • Touch or mouse: drag a card or a run, or tap a card to send it to its best spot. Tap the stock to draw.
  • Keyboard: arrows move between piles, Enter or Space picks up and places, Z undoes, H hints.
