Stock the spice rack

Swap two neighbours to line up three or more. Four in a line makes a grinder that clears a line, five makes a jar that clears one whole kind. Beat the target before the moves run out.

  • Touch and mouse Drag a spice toward a neighbour, or tap one and then its neighbour.
  • Keyboard Arrows move, Enter or Space picks, then an arrow swaps. H for a hint.
