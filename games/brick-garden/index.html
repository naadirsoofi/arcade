Tend the garden

Bounce the ball off your plank to clear every plot. Soil breaks in one hit, seed plots sprout and then bloom, and stone edging never breaks.

Catch what falls: the watering can widens the plank, the bee adds a ball, and the snail slows the ball down.

  • Keyboard Arrow keys or A and D to move, Space to launch, P or Escape to pause.
