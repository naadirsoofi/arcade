Lead the caravan

Steer to the date clusters: each one adds a camel and scores two points per camel in the train, and the train speeds up as it grows. Rocks, the map edge and your own camels end the run.

When an oasis appears, reach it in time to trade your last three camels for 40 points and a shorter, slower train.

  • Keyboard Arrow keys or WASD to steer. P or Escape to pause.
  • Touch Swipe on the dunes or tap the arrow buttons.
